The one idea

Over a lifetime, costs are the variable you control. So we start there.

Nobody can reliably tell you which fund will win next year — including us, and including the manager charging 1% to try. But a fund’s expense ratio is knowable today, it compounds against you every single year, and it is the single best predictor of long-run results that money can buy. Every review on this site leads with cost, explains what you’re actually buying, and tells you who the fund fits. Then it gets out of your way.
