The bottom line

DODGX is the active fund to own if you own any, with shareholder-aligned structure and a real after-fee record. But VVIAX delivers the value style for 0.05% versus 0.51%, and over full cycles the index keeps the race close. Conviction in the strategy justifies DODGX; default rationality justifies VVIAX.

About DODGX

If you forced this site to recommend one active stock fund, Dodge & Cox Stock would make the shortlist every time. The firm is everything the industry usually isn't: privately owned, one office, no marketing budget, committee-managed so no star can leave, fees around 0.51% when value peers charge double, and turnover so low the portfolio reads like a will rather than a trading blotter.

About VVIAX

Value investing's pitch is ancient and simple — pay less than things are worth — and VVIAX industrializes it: a few hundred large companies screened for low prices relative to fundamentals, no storytelling, 0.05%. Banks, healthcare, energy, industrials; the unglamorous economy that pays dividends while tech gets the headlines.
