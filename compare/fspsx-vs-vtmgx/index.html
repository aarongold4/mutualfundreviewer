The bottom line

FSPSX (0.035%, MSCI EAFE, large/mid only) is cheaper; VTMGX (0.08%) adds developed small caps for slightly more. The coverage difference is minor and the cost difference is trivial. As ever, hold the one your brokerage offers without friction.

About FSPSX

FSPSX tracks MSCI EAFE — the oldest, most-quoted international benchmark — which means developed markets only: no China, no India, no emerging markets, and no Canada either, a quirk of the index's 1969 design. Around 700 large and mid caps from Japan, the UK, and continental Europe at 0.035%.

About VTMGX

VTMGX is total-international minus the emerging markets: Japan, the UK, Europe, Canada, Australia, and the rest of the developed world, about 4,000 stocks at 0.08%. Investors choose it over VTIAX either because they're skeptical of emerging markets' governance and volatility, or because they want to size the EM allocation separately with a dedicated fund.
