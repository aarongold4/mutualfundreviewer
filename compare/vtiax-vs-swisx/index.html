The bottom line

VTIAX is the more complete one-fund answer, including emerging markets and small caps. SWISX suits investors who want developed markets only or size EM separately. Know what you're buying: these track different slices of the world, not the same slice at different prices.

About VTIAX

Whether to own international stocks at all is one of the few arguments that still divides otherwise-agreeing index investors. Bogle himself was famously lukewarm on the idea; Vanguard's own target-date funds put roughly 40% of equities abroad. If you land on the 'yes' side, VTIAX is the cleanest single answer: every developed and emerging market outside the US, large caps through small, in one fund at 0.09%.

About SWISX

SWISX is Schwab's house international fund, and the key thing to know is what it leaves out: tracking MSCI EAFE means developed markets only — no emerging markets, and (EAFE quirk) no Canada. Around 700 stocks from Japan, Europe, and Australia at 0.06%. For Schwab loyalists building a three-fund portfolio, it's the standard international piece.
